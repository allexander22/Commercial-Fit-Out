## Project overview

This example illustrates the type of programme A2 Commercial Fit Out delivers for office clients relocating within Kent. A professional services tenant required a full Cat B fit out across two floors of a Maidstone town-centre office building, moving from an outdated open-plan layout to a mix of meeting rooms, breakout space and agile desking.

## Scope

- Full partition layout across two floors, including four acoustic meeting rooms
- Suspended ceiling replacement with integrated lighting grid
- New staff washroom and kitchen tiling
- Cabling containment coordination with the client's IT contractor
- Out-of-hours finishing trades in the final week to protect the client's moving date

## Outcome

Delivered to a fixed price agreed at contract stage, with in-house dry lining, ceiling and tiling trades keeping the seven-week programme on track despite a compressed final fortnight. The client relocated 90 staff over a single weekend with no disruption to their ongoing operations.
