## Project Overview

This example represents the kind of retail fit out programme A2 Commercial Fit Out runs regularly inside occupied London shopping centres, where the surrounding units continue trading throughout the works.

## Scope

- Shell-and-core unit fit out to trading-ready condition
- New partition layout for back-of-house and staff welfare areas
- Full washroom tiling package
- Ceiling and lighting grid installation
- All disruptive trades scheduled outside centre trading hours under centre-management booking rules

## Outcome

Working to a fixed price agreed before mobilisation, the in-house trades team ran all noisy and disruptive work — partition demolition, floor preparation, ceiling replacement — during out-of-hours and overnight slots booked with centre management. The unit was handed back ready for the retailer's own shopfitting programme on the agreed date, with no disruption reported by neighbouring tenants.
