## Project Overview

This example illustrates a typical industrial fit out on an Essex business park, where a logistics tenant needed new mezzanine office and welfare space built into an operational warehouse unit.

## Scope

- New mezzanine-level office fit out with partitioned meeting rooms and open-plan desking
- Suspended ceiling and lighting grid throughout the new office area
- Staff welfare facilities including washrooms and kitchen, fully tiled
- Fire-rated separation between office and warehouse zones to current Building Regulations
- Phased delivery to keep the warehouse floor operational throughout

## Outcome

Delivered to a fixed price with in-house trades running the dry lining, ceiling and tiling packages in parallel, the nine-week programme kept disruption to the client's live warehouse operation to a minimum. The finished space gave the client head-office-standard facilities within their existing industrial footprint.
