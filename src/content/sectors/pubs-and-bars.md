Every week a pub or bar is closed for works is lost revenue, so programme certainty matters more here than in almost any other sector. As a **pub and bar fit out company**, A2 Commercial Fit Out builds every hospitality programme around the client's trading calendar — working evenings, overnight and across closure windows so venues reopen on the date promised.

Our in-house trades — dry lining, suspended ceilings, tape & jointing and tiling — cover the bulk of a typical hospitality fit out, from bar-back partitioning and ceiling grids to washroom and cellar-area tiling. Keeping these trades in-house means we can compress programmes without the coordination delays that come from managing multiple subcontractor chains, and every job is quoted at a fixed price before we start, so licensees and operators know the full cost up front.

Pub and bar fit out work we deliver includes:

- Bar servery and back-of-house reconfiguration
- Washroom refurbishment and re-tiling
- Ceiling and lighting grid replacement
- Fire safety compartmentation between public and back-of-house areas
- Full refits during planned closure periods, including out-of-hours finishing trades

We work with landlords, operators and main contractors on both single-site refits and multi-site rollout programmes, always with the same fixed-price, in-house-trade approach that keeps hospitality clients trading again as quickly as possible.
