A turnkey fit out means a client hands over an approved design and receives a finished, handover-ready space back — with a single point of responsibility in between. A2 Commercial Fit Out operates as a **turnkey fit out contractor**, taking a completed design pack from an architect or designer and delivering the full build to specification, on a fixed price, without the client having to manage multiple trade packages themselves.

We are a delivery contractor, not a design house: we build to your design, your specification and your Building Regulations approvals, and we take responsibility for coordinating every trade package needed to get there. Because dry lining, suspended ceilings, tape & jointing and tiling sit in-house, the packages that most commonly cause programme slippage — internal partitions, ceiling grids, finishes — stay under our direct control rather than a subcontractor's.

A typical turnkey scope covers:

- Mobilisation and pre-start programme planning against the approved design
- Full internal build-out: partitions, ceilings, M&E first and second fix coordination, finishes
- Compliance sign-off — Building Regulations, fire safety, CDM 2015 principal contractor duties where appointed
- Snagging, commissioning and handover documentation
- Single fixed-price contract covering the full scope, agreed before mobilisation

Turnkey delivery suits landlords, main contractors and end-user clients who want one contract and one accountable delivery partner, rather than managing a dozen separate trade relationships. Our out-of-hours capability also means turnkey programmes for occupied buildings can continue without disrupting existing tenants or operations.
