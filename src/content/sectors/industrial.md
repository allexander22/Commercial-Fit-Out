Industrial units bring a different set of pressures to a fit out programme: larger floorplates, mixed-use requirements (office, welfare and warehouse space under one roof), and clients who need the building operational on a fixed date. A2 Commercial Fit Out works as an **industrial fit out contractor** across London, Kent, Essex and the wider South East, delivering everything from mezzanine office pods to full warehouse-to-workspace conversions.

Because our dry lining, suspended ceiling, tape & jointing and tiling trades sit in-house, we can run large floorplates in parallel rather than queuing behind subcontractor availability — a common cause of slippage on industrial programmes. Every quote is fixed-price before work starts, so budget holders have cost certainty across the full scope, not just the headline build.

Typical industrial fit out scopes we deliver include:

- Warehouse mezzanine offices and welfare facilities
- Light-industrial unit partitioning and compartmentation
- Workshop-to-office conversions within existing industrial shells
- Fire-rated separation between industrial and office zones, compliant with current Building Regulations
- Out-of-hours and phased delivery where a unit needs to stay partially operational

Industrial fit outs often run alongside live logistics or production operations, so out-of-hours and weekend working is frequently the difference between a smooth handover and a disrupted client. We plan phased access with site management from day one, and our in-house trades mean we can flex working hours without renegotiating subcontractor terms.
