A retail opening date is usually fixed months in advance — stock deliveries, staff start dates and marketing are all built around it, so a **shop fitout** contractor has to hit programme every time. A2 Commercial Fit Out delivers retail unit fit outs across London, Kent, Essex and the South East with fixed-price quotes and in-house trades that keep fast-turnaround programmes on track.

Shop units frequently sit within occupied shopping centres or high streets, which means working around centre management hours, shared service corridors and neighbouring tenants. Our out-of-hours capability lets us run noisy or disruptive trades — partitioning, ceiling work, tiling — outside centre trading hours, so the wider scheme stays on programme without breaching landlord or centre-management conditions.

Retail fit out work we deliver includes:

- Shell-and-core to trading-ready unit fit out
- Partition, ceiling and shopfront-adjacent internal works
- Washroom, staff area and back-of-house build-out
- Floor and wall tiling to retailer specification
- Fast-turnaround programmes aligned to fixed opening dates

Whether it's a single independent unit or a rollout across multiple retail locations, every shop fitout is quoted at a fixed price before mobilisation, with a realistic programme built around your opening date, not ours.
