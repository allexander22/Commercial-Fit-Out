Bespoke fit out work is where a specification is the whole point — reception joinery, feature walls, tailored storage and non-standard finishes that a template fit out can't provide. A2 Commercial Fit Out delivers **bespoke fit out** packages built precisely to an agreed specification, whether that's a single feature installation within a wider scheme or a fully bespoke commercial interior.

Our approach is deliberately a delivery one: we work from your specification, drawings and material schedules and build to match — we don't redesign on the fly. That discipline matters most on bespoke work, where small deviations from spec are expensive to unpick later. Our in-house dry lining, suspended ceiling, tape & jointing and tiling trades let us hold tolerances and finish quality consistently across a bespoke scope, and every bespoke package is still quoted at a fixed price before work begins.

Bespoke fit out work we deliver includes:

- Feature ceilings, bulkheads and lighting coves
- Bespoke partition and screen installations
- Specialist tiling schemes for washrooms, wet areas and feature walls
- Reception and back-of-house joinery coordination alongside our trade teams
- Small-batch or single-unit bespoke installations within a larger commercial scheme

Because we hold no minimum project value, bespoke fit out enquiries range from a single feature installation to a full bespoke commercial interior — the same fixed-price, in-house-trade approach applies either way.
