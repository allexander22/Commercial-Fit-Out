Fitting out a floor or unit in London usually means working inside a live, occupied building — shared lifts, loading-bay booking windows, building management sign-off and neighbouring tenants who need to keep operating. A2 Commercial Fit Out delivers **commercial fit out in London** with that reality built into every programme, not treated as an afterthought.

Out-of-hours working is often the only realistic way to deliver disruptive trades — partition demolition, floor preparation, ceiling grid replacement — in a building that's occupied nine-to-five. We plan evening and weekend phases around building management booking systems, lift permits and fire-alarm isolation procedures from the outset, so the programme survives contact with a real building rather than assuming an empty shell.

Because our dry lining, suspended ceiling, tape & jointing and tiling trades are in-house, we can compress site time in buildings where every extra week carries real cost — whether that's rent on an unoccupied floor or lost trading days for a retail unit. Every quote is fixed-price before mobilisation, which matters in London where variable-cost overruns can quickly erode a project's budget.

We work across Central and Greater London on office fit out and refurbishment, retail units, and healthcare fit out, alongside architects, designers, landlords and main contractors who need a delivery partner comfortable operating inside occupied commercial buildings. Building Regulations compliance, fire safety and CDM 2015 duties are managed as standard on every London programme, regardless of project size.
