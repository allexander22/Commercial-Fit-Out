A2 Commercial Fit Out is based in Maidstone, in the heart of Kent, which means a genuinely fast response across the county rather than a contractor travelling in from further afield. We deliver **commercial fit out across Kent** — office, retail, industrial and healthcare projects — for clients from Maidstone, Ashford, Canterbury, Tunbridge Wells, Dartford and the wider county.

Being locally based shortens the distance between a first enquiry and a site visit, and it means our in-house trades — dry lining, suspended ceilings, tape & jointing and tiling — can mobilise quickly without the logistics overhead of a contractor based outside the county. That local footprint also supports out-of-hours and weekend working where a Kent client needs a space handed back with minimal disruption to their operation.

Kent's commercial stock ranges from town-centre office buildings and retail units to industrial estates around the M20 and M2 corridors, and we deliver across all of it — with the same fixed-price approach and no minimum project value, so a small refurbishment gets the same commitment as a full floor fit out.

Every Kent project is delivered to current Building Regulations and fire safety standards, with CDM 2015 duties managed appropriately for the scale of works. Our home base in Maidstone means ongoing client relationships across the county are easy to sustain — from initial fit out through to future refurbishment or expansion projects.
