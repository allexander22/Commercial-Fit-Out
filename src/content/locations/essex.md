Essex's commercial landscape is shaped by its business parks and industrial estates — from the M11 and A12 corridors into Chelmsford, Basildon, Colchester and towards the Thames Gateway — and A2 Commercial Fit Out delivers **commercial fit out across Essex** into exactly that mix of office, industrial and retail stock.

Business park office fit outs in Essex often come with shared landlord conditions and service-charge considerations that need coordinating alongside the build itself, while industrial units bring larger floorplates and mixed office/warehouse requirements. We plan both scenarios the same way: a fixed-price quote agreed before mobilisation, and in-house dry lining, suspended ceiling, tape & jointing and tiling trades that keep the programme under our direct control rather than a subcontractor's availability.

Typical Essex projects include:

- Business park office fit out and Cat A/Cat B refurbishment
- Industrial unit office and welfare-facility fit out
- Retail unit fit out on Essex high streets and retail parks
- Out-of-hours delivery where a business park unit needs to remain partially operational

Whether the project is a single Essex business park suite or a larger industrial fit out, we apply the same fixed-price, in-house-trade delivery model — with no minimum project value — and manage Building Regulations, fire safety and CDM 2015 compliance as standard throughout.
