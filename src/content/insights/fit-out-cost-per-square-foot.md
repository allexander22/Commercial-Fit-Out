"How much does a fit out cost per square foot?" is usually the first question a client asks, and the honest answer is always "it depends on specification" — but that's not a very useful starting point on its own, so here's a proper breakdown of what actually drives the number.

## The three biggest cost drivers

**Specification level.** A basic Cat B fit out with standard partitioning, carpet tile and painted finishes costs a fraction of a high-spec scheme with feature joinery, acoustic ceiling treatments and premium flooring. Specification is the single biggest swing factor in any quote.

**Existing building condition.** A modern Cat A shell with services already distributed to the floor costs less to fit out than an older building needing M&E upgrades, additional fire compartmentation, or accessibility improvements before the visible fit out work can even begin.

**Working conditions.** Occupied buildings requiring out-of-hours or weekend working, restricted access (shared lifts, limited loading-bay windows, city-centre delivery restrictions) and tight programmes all add cost compared with an empty shell with unrestricted access.

## What's typically included at each level

**Basic** specification suits cost-conscious tenants who need a functional, compliant space quickly: standard partitioning, suspended ceiling tiles, carpet tile flooring, painted walls and standard sanitaryware. This is a common choice for short-lease industrial office pods or straightforward retail back-of-house areas.

**Mid-range** specification is the most common choice for established businesses fitting out a head office or a customer-facing retail unit: better acoustic separation, some feature lighting, upgraded flooring in key zones, and branded reception finishes.

**High-spec** specification suits flagship offices, premium retail and healthcare environments where finish quality, acoustic performance and durability all matter: bespoke joinery, feature ceilings, premium flooring throughout and enhanced M&E provision.
